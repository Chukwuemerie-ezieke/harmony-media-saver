Welcome to Harmony Media Saver

Save the lesson videos and audio your school already shares, straight to your computer.

How to use it

  1. Open a page you are allowed to save media from, such as your school's learning platform.
  2. Press play if the video or audio only loads when it starts.
  3. Click the Harmony Media Saver icon in the toolbar. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu so it is always visible.
  4. Click Download, or tick several files and click Download selected.

What it saves

  • Video files such as MP4, WebM and MOV
  • Audio files such as MP3, M4A and WAV
  • Files a website already serves as a normal file

What it will not do

  • Download from YouTube, Netflix and other streaming services
  • Bypass DRM, logins or paywalls
  • Join streaming playlists (HLS/DASH) into a video

Why is nothing showing?

  • The page must start with http or https. Chrome settings pages, the Web Store and files opened from your computer are not scanned.
  • Press play, wait a moment, then click Scan.
  • The site may be on the blocked list, or not on your allowlist. Check Options.
  • Many modern players only stream in pieces and never expose a single file. Those cannot be saved.
  • If the page loaded before the extension was installed, reload the page.

Everything stays on this computer: no account, no analytics, no servers.

Only save media you are authorized to download.
