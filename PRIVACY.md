# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 August 2026

Harmony Media Saver is a local browser extension. It does not operate a backend and does not send browsing data to Harmony Digital Consults Ltd.

## Data the extension handles

- Media URLs, filenames, content types, and sizes observed on the current tab
- Settings you configure (folder name, blocklist, allowlist, save-as preference)
- A local download history of files you chose to save

## Where data is stored

All of that data stays in Chrome storage on your device (`chrome.storage.session` for live detections and `chrome.storage.local` for settings and history).

## What we do not collect

- No account
- No analytics
- No crash telemetry
- No upload of page content, cookies, or media files to our servers

## Network access

The extension only contacts a remote host when you start a download. Chrome then requests that media URL directly, the same way a normal browser download works.

## Your controls

Use the Options page to clear history, edit the blocklist, or enable allowlist-only mode. Removing the extension deletes its local storage.
